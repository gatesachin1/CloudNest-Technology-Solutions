FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a typical project take?

A business website is typically live within 7–14 days. A DevOps setup or AWS migration depends on complexity — most projects complete within 2–6 weeks. We'll give you a detailed timeline after the discovery call.

Do you work with startups or only large businesses?

We work with everyone — solo founders, SMBs, startups, and larger enterprises. Our pricing is structured to match different stages of growth. The free consultation is the best way to figure out which engagement model fits your needs.

Do we need to be in Pune to work with you?

Not at all. We work with clients across India and internationally, fully remote. Most of our collaboration happens via video calls, email, WhatsApp, and shared documentation. Location is never a barrier.

What happens after the project is delivered?

All projects include a handover period with documentation and training. We also offer ongoing maintenance and support retainers so you have a dedicated team available when you need updates, monitoring, or new features.
