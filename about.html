About Cloudnest

Why We Started
Cloudnest

Cloudnest Technology Solutions was founded with one observation: Indian SMBs were spending lakhs on custom software and websites, but the underlying infrastructure was fragile — shared hosting that went down at peak traffic, zero monitoring, no backups, and no path to scale.

We set out to change that. By combining cloud-native AWS architecture with proper DevOps practices, we give small and medium businesses the same infrastructure reliability that large enterprises take for granted.

Today, we help businesses across India — from Pune clinics to Mumbai e-commerce brands — run on infrastructure that is fast, secure, monitored 24/7, and built to grow with them. Every system we build is automated, version-controlled, and documented so there are no surprises at 2 AM.

Our approach is simple: treat every client's infrastructure like it's our own. That means we over-communicate, we automate relentlessly, and we never ship something we wouldn't be comfortable supporting at midnight.

Our Values
